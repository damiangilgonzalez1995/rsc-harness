# ADR-NNN: title that states the decision, not the topic

- **Status**: Accepted | Proposed | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decider**: name
- **Context**: spec, PR, email, or conversation it originates from (optional)

## Context and problem

What was there, why it didn't work, and what effects it produced. Verifiable
facts (files, observed behavior, PR number), not a session narrative.
Two or three paragraphs at most.

## Decision

**D1.** What is done, in the present tense, with the concrete piece of the system that changes.
One sentence of why when it isn't obvious.

**D2.** Next decision. Each D is cited separately ("ADR-NNN D2") and can be
superseded separately.

> **Amendment YYYY-MM-DD.** Only if needed later: what changes relative to
> the previous D and the trade-off accepted.

## Consequences

- What is gained.
- What it costs or loses, stated plainly.
- What test, guard, or check protects it, if any.

## Discarded alternatives

- **Alternative**: why not. Enough that no one proposes it again
  without reading this.
